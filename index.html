About

From a lab possibility to a module that runs on the road

I research autonomous driving perception at the Vehicle Intelligence Laboratory (VILAB), Graduate School of Automobile and Mobility, Kookmin University. I completed the Ph.D. coursework in August 2026 and expect to receive the degree in August 2027.

I treat two questions as one problem: how to fuse camera, radar and LiDAR in BEV space, and how to make that model run on a vehicle's edge board within a fixed time budget. Rather than reporting a single aggregate score, I break performance down into the regimes where a model actually fails — night, rain, long range — and work out why.

GaussianDT — camera–radar Gaussian BEV detection and tracking

To address both grid discretization error and the detection–tracking split, I defined an augmented 3D Gaussian carrying a motion vector and an identity embedding. Radar Doppler is used as a velocity prior rather than a plain input channel, anchoring velocity estimation to a physical measurement.

  • nuScenes mAP 33.6
  • AMOTA 0.372
  • ID-Switch 1432 → 1050
  • Best Paper Award Finalist
2025 — 2026
First author
IEEE Access

SegMam — Pseudo-LiDAR with Cross-Mamba attention for BEV segmentation

I reformulated Transformer cross-attention as Mamba's selective scan, yielding a Query–Value two-stream block with no explicit Key projection. Pseudo-LiDAR point clouds derived from monocular depth serve as the value stream. No real LiDAR or radar is used anywhere in the pipeline.

  • Drivable Area IoU 81.6
  • Vehicle IoU 50.0
  • Night Map IoU +13.6
  • Rain +6.0
  • Code and checkpoints released
2025 — 2026
Co-author
KSAE

Real-time BEV on edge devices — ONNX, TensorRT, Jetson

Instead of stopping at desktop numbers, I exported the model to ONNX, quantized it to FP16, built a TensorRT engine and deployed it on Jetson boards. Measurements confirmed that the same model differs by a factor of two in latency across board generations.

  • IoU held at about 72%
  • AGX Orin 44 ms
  • AGX Thor 22 ms
  • vs. 352 ms baseline
2024 — present
National project
KEIT

Vehicle data pipeline and ROS perception module

I helped convert a Sonata into a data collection platform (6 cameras, 1 LiDAR, GPS, IMU) and ran polygon ground-truth labeling for drivable area. Model output is converted into an ego-centric occupancy grid published at 10 Hz to the planning module, with the interface agreed across four institutions and validated in a certified evaluation.

  • 0.3 m · 200×120 grid
  • nav_msgs/OccupancyGrid
  • 119 scenes labeled
  • KIAPI certification
