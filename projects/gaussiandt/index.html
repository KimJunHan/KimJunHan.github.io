GaussianDT

Gaussian Splatting Optimization for Object Detection and Tracking with Camera–Radar Fusion

First author 2025 — 2026 AVEC 2026, Tsukuba, Japan

Overview

GaussianDT addresses two fundamental problems in BEV perception simultaneously: grid discretization error inherent in voxel-based representations, and the conventional separation between detection and tracking pipelines.

The core idea is to define an augmented 3D Gaussian primitive that carries not only spatial occupancy but also a motion vector and an identity embedding. This allows the model to detect, localize, and associate objects in a single forward pass without relying on post-hoc Hungarian matching.

Approach

Instead of treating radar Doppler as just another input channel, GaussianDT uses it as a velocity prior — anchoring velocity estimation to a physical measurement rather than learning it purely from visual appearance. Each Gaussian carries:

  • 3D position (x, y, z)
  • Covariance (shape)
  • Motion vector (vx, vy)
  • Identity embedding
  • Radar Doppler prior

The camera features are lifted into BEV space and fused with radar returns. The Gaussian representation avoids the spatial quantization artifacts of grid-based methods, enabling more precise localization especially at long range.
